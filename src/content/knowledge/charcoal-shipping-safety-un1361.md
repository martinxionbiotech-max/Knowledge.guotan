# Charcoal Shipping Safety: UN 1361 and Why Your Supplier Must Declare It

Most first-time charcoal buyers assume the product ships like any other cargo.
It does not. Coconut shell charcoal — hookah cubes included — is a regulated
dangerous good at sea, and the rules tightened in 2025-2026.

## The classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| UN number | 1361 |
| Proper shipping name | Carbon, animal or vegetable origin |
| Hazard class | 4.2 — substances liable to spontaneous combustion |
| Packing group | III |

Charcoal can self-heat: fresh product, trapped moisture and poor ventilation
can raise the temperature inside a sealed container over a long voyage and, in
rare cases, reach ignition. Public industry analysis links 68 container-ship
fires between 2015 and 2022 to charcoal cargo.

## What changed (IMDG Amendment 42-24)

The old exemption path — test the product with the N.4 self-heating test and
ship outside dangerous-goods rules (Special Provision 925) — has been
withdrawn. Since the 2025/2026 IMDG Code:

- **All** charcoal of animal or vegetable origin ships as UN 1361, Class 4.2,
  regardless of any prior self-heating test result.
- Bulk carriage in containers without packaging is no longer an option.
- Shipments move under Special Provision 978 process controls: preparation,
  packing and declaration requirements for every consignment.
- Major carriers (Maersk, Hapag-Lloyd, CMA CGM among them) began enforcing
  the rules during 2025.

## What this means for a buyer

1. **Declaration is a competence signal.** A supplier who declares UN 1361
   openly is set up to ship correctly. A supplier who offers to "save money"
   by shipping undeclared is proposing an illegal booking that can be seized,
   fined or refused — and has caused fires at sea.
2. **Packaging carries dual labels.** Trade labels (brand, weight, origin)
   plus dangerous-goods marks: UN 1361 and the Class 4.2 diamond on every
   bag or carton, with placards on the container.
3. **Ask for the self-heating test on file.** Reputable exporters keep current
   self-heating characterisation results and can produce them when a carrier
   asks.
4. **Verify before paying.** Confirm with your own freight forwarder that the
   booking is declared as UN 1361 / Class 4.2 with the correct documentation
   — do not rely on the supplier's word alone.
